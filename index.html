Sampling Sites —

Lauriuchi et al. 2026 sampled three field-campaign sites per reservoir, but restricted final model calibration/validation to the near-dam site only (Ag1 / Rb1) — matching APA/SNIRH's own official monitoring convention. This automated pipeline follows the same restriction: only Ag1 and Rb1 feed the Chl-a / Secchi Depth estimates shown in this dashboard. Other sites are shown for geographic context only.
